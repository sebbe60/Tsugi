# Tsugi — MALSync Fork

## Summary

A private-feeling anime companion with a full-tab library, watch history, discovery, and automatic episode tracking.

## Description

Tsugi is a personal Firefox extension built from the open-source MALSync project. It turns your connected anime list into a desktop-style library where you can see what to continue, when the next episode releases in your timezone, what is upcoming, and what you have watched.

### Highlights

- Full-tab Watching, Upcoming, Planned, History, Discover, and My sites views.
- Automatic episode tracking on supported sites, with visible sync confirmation, local recovery, and playback checkpoints.
- A quiet in-player tracking indicator that shows detected progress.
- Release schedules in your selected timezone with a 24-hour clock option.
- History grouped by watch date, including unfinished episodes that can be resumed.
- Discovery for sequels, side stories, OVAs, and related titles you have not watched.
- Your chosen streaming sites in one place, with quick links that open in a new tab.

Tsugi supports the list services and sites inherited from MALSync, plus personal site configuration. It is independent from MALSync and is not an official MALSync release.
